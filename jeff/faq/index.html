Jeff's F.A.Q.

Who is Jeff?

Jefferson (or Jeff) is an American Domestic Shorthair who lives in southern NH. He was adopted from a shelter on November 30th, 2020, and is approximately 8 years old.

Where did the name "Jefferson" come from?

We don't know. Jeff came to us from the shelter with this name. We did give him his middle name: Elmer. Because he looks like he's been glued together.

Can I "Meet Jeff"?

Yeah, you already have. Go back to the "Meet Jeff" page if you'd like to see him again!

Okay, but can I actually "Meet Jeff"?

No, sorry. Jefferson is far too shy for that. Plus, he's an indoor cat. Jeff will be giving no autographs at this time, thank you.

How many pictures of Jeff are there?

On this website? 150. In total? Many, many more than that.

Why are there specifically 150 pictures of Jeff?

100 pictures didn't feel like enough to convey the true essence of Jeff!

Are you going to add more pictures of Jeff?

Probably not. This website is hosted on a service that only allows so much storage space. I've already had to restrict myself.

Who is are you?

Jeff was adopted by my mother. I suppose that makes me Jefferson's... brother?

Why did you make this website dedicated to Jeff?

Jefferson is a cat who is very easy to take ridiculous photos of. I also love him very much, and wanted to share him with more people.

Did you put up the "Meet Jeff" stickers?

Some of them, yes. But I have also given a number of them to friends and family to put up, as well, with instruction to either only put them up with permission whenever possible, or alongside other stickers in a public art installation. If this sticker was put up someplace it shouldn't have been, I apologize. You can remove the sticker by catching the corner with a blade and peeling slowly. It's vinyl, it should peel off in one piece. Leftover residue can be cleaned with hydrogen peroxide.

Where were the stickers printed?

I used a service called StickerApp. They're pretty cool.

What font is this?

This is "Matcha Mint", as long as you don't have "simplify font" enabled. I'll add the artist here later.

Was generative AI used during this project?

Regretfully, YES. But... All pictures and designs present on this website were all either sourced by myself, my family, or real human beings. Generative AI has only been used during the debugging process for JavaScript and CSS. I'm not a web designer. This website is practically held together with masking tape and dreams. And cat fur. A lot of cat fur...
